Welcome to the Zyn Demo!

Zyn is a small JS audio synthesizer which clocks in at around 8.8kb (~3.2kb gzipped). You can read all about it on GitHub here.

This interactive tool allows you to explore procedurally generated synthesizer instruments. Use your computer keyboard, MIDI controller, or the on-screen piano to play notes and experiment with different instrument seeds. There are 10 instrument types (pad, lead, bass, key, pluck, bell, string, drum, perc, fx) determined by the last digit of the seed.

  • Play notes: Use QWERTY keys on your keyboard (Q is middle C), click the on-screen piano, or connect a MIDI controller
  • Change octave: Use the dropdown or Page Up/Down keys (also applies to MIDI input)
  • Adjust volume: Use the volume slider (0-500%) to control output level
  • Generate instruments: Select a type and click "Generate Random", or enter a seed number directly
  • Browse seeds: Use + and - keys to cycle through instrument seeds
  • Stop sound: Press Escape or click "Stop All"
  • Save presets: Enter a name and click "Save Current" to save instruments you like
  • Manage presets: Presets save octave and volume settings, and can be edited in-place
  • Export/Import: Back up your presets to a JSON file or share them with others
  • MIDI support: Click "Enable MIDI" to use a connected MIDI keyboard with velocity sensitivity and pedal support (sustain, sostenuto, soft)
Notice: As this synth generates random sounds, some may be unexpected or harsh. Please listen at a safe and comfortable volume to protect your hearing and enjoy the experience.
